The Qur'an Evaluation Engine

A complete guide for leadership, admin, teachers and parents.

Why this document exists

Every term, every student at AMQM sits for three Qur'an evaluations. The result of each one moves the student forward in their Hifz journey and feeds into their report card, parent view, and finally the completion certificate at the end of the two year programme.

Because so much hinges on these evaluations being honest and consistent, this document is the single reference anyone in the school can turn to. It explains what the system does, who is responsible for which step, how the final percentage is calculated, and the small handful of rules that keep every student's record truthful across years.

The yearly rhythm, in one picture

The AMQM Hifz programme is a two year journey. Each year has three terms. Each term carries three evaluations. The engine runs the same cycle nine times a year, eighteen times across the full programme.

1 year = 3 terms 1 term = 3 evaluations (Eval 1, Eval 2, Eval 3) Programme = 2 years = 18 evaluations in total

Two memorization directions exist at the school, and the engine treats them equally.

  • Baqarah to Nas. Student starts at Al Baqarah ayah 1 and moves forward through the mushaf, finishing at An Nas.
  • Nas to Baqarah. Student starts at An Nas and moves backward through the mushaf, finishing at Al Baqarah ayah 1.

The one source of truth. Dates.

All evaluation dates live in the School Calendar page. Nowhere else. Admin opens the Calendar once per term, sets the opening and closing date and time for each of the three evaluations, saves, and the rest of the system reads from that single record.

If the admin needs to extend or bring forward a date later, editing it on the Calendar page automatically updates every open evaluation campaign in the system. Teacher countdowns rebase on their next refresh. There is no second place that stores evaluation dates and no risk of two values clashing.

The lifecycle at a glance

1School Calendar

Admin sets the open and close dates for Eval 1, Eval 2, Eval 3 of each term, in advance.

2Admin opens the round

On the Evaluations page the admin presses Open on the round that is due. The system reads the Calendar date and sets up draft rows for every student.

3Teacher enters scores

Each teacher opens their Evaluations page, sees the countdown and the list of students assigned to them, and fills in the stopping position plus five quality notes per student.

4Teacher submits

When the class is done, the teacher presses Submit all. Rows leave the teacher and arrive in the admin review queue.

5Admin reviews

Admin approves each submission or returns it with a reason for the teacher to correct and resubmit.

6Student profile advances

On approval, the student's official Qur'an position advances to the stopping point. That becomes the starting position for the next evaluation.

The chain is unbroken by design. Eval 1 stop becomes Eval 2 start. Eval 2 stop becomes Eval 3 start. Eval 3 stop becomes the next term's Eval 1 start. No one has to carry numbers forward by hand.

How the percentage is calculated

The headline number on each evaluation reflects how far the student has memorized in the Holy Qur'an at the stopping position they submitted. It is not a teacher opinion. It is not a rubric average. It is the student's real place in the mushaf, expressed as a percentage of the full 6,236 ayahs.

For a Baqarah to Nas student

score % = (ayahs memorized from Al Baqarah 1 onward) divided by 6,236 times 100

For a Nas to Baqarah student

score % = ( 6,236 minus current global ayah ordinal ) divided by 6,236 times 100

A worked example

Abbas Lawan Bello is a Nas to Baqarah student currently at At Tahrim, ayah 12. At Tahrim is Surah 66. Counting from Al Baqarah ayah 1 through to At Tahrim ayah 12 gives a global ordinal of about 5,246 within the mushaf.

  • Ayahs memorized backward from An Nas: 6,236 minus 5,246 equals about 990 ayahs.
  • Percentage: 990 divided by 6,236, multiplied by 100, equals about 15.9 percent.

That same 15.9 percent appears on his student profile and on his report card. One source of truth, used everywhere.

What the five rubrics are for

Every evaluation entry has five quality fields the teacher scores from one to five.

CodeMeaningWhat the teacher is judging
MemMemorizationHow well the student has retained the ayahs committed to memory, with minimal errors or hesitation.
AccAccuracyCorrect pronunciation of each letter, with proper makhraj.
FluFluencyPace, confidence and smoothness. A fluent reciter sounds settled, not jerky.
TajTajweedApplication of tajweed rules: ghunnah, idgham, madd, qalqalah and similar.
RetRetentionHow much of the earlier memorized portion the student still holds, when asked to recite backwards or sampled at random.

These five numbers are stored with every evaluation and reach the admin at review time. They act as a coaching signal, not as the headline score. The headline score is Qur'an completion. The quality signal tells the admin and the teacher where to focus support.

Direction rules the engine enforces

The system refuses to accept a stopping position that has not moved in the student's declared memorization direction. The field will simply refuse to submit, and the row will show the note "Position not moved" in red.

DirectionStopping position must be
Baqarah to Nas (forward)Later than the starting position in the mushaf.
Nas to Baqarah (reverse)Earlier than the starting position in the mushaf.
This guard exists because the system computes Qur'an completion from the raw position. A wrong direction would corrupt the percentage, the report card and the next evaluation's starting point. The engine therefore refuses to accept a row that violates the rule.

What approval triggers

When the admin presses Approve on a submitted row, four things happen at once inside a single transaction.

  1. The evaluation record is marked approved, with the approving admin's identity and timestamp.
  2. The student's official position on their profile moves to the stopping position: current surah, current ayah, current page and current hizb all advance together.
  3. The report card is updated. Parents and the student's family will see the new position the next time they open it.
  4. An audit log entry is written with a complete before and after snapshot. Nothing is ever silently lost.

If this approval was the student's Eval 3 of Term 3 in Year 2, and the stopping position reaches the end of their memorization journey (An Nas ayah 6 for a forward reader, Al Baqarah ayah 1 for a reverse reader), the system automatically marks the student graduated, issues their completion certificate and creates their alumni profile row.

Guarantees

  • Every active student is evaluated every term, three times.
  • Each evaluation's starting position is the previous one's approved stopping position. No one carries numbers forward by hand.
  • The final percentage reflects memorization through the Holy Qur'an, not teacher opinion.
  • Memorization direction is respected individually for every student.
  • Every change, from teacher submission to admin approval or override, is logged in the audit trail with full before and after snapshots.
  • Reports, dashboards, parent views, and completion certificates all read from the same stored position on the student record. The source of truth never drifts.
  • A teacher can only edit evaluation rows for students in the classes they are currently assigned to.
  • Admins can only change a student's position through the audit logged profile update, never directly in the database.

Common scenarios

A new teacher takes over a class in the middle of the term

Admin reassigns the class on the Classes page. On the new teacher's next refresh of the Evaluations page, they immediately see every pending row for every student in that class. The system matches teachers through the live class teacher link, not a frozen snapshot from the moment the round was opened.

A student transfers to a different class in the middle of the term

Admin edits the student profile and changes their class. The next evaluation they are part of will use the new class, which means the new teacher will receive their row. Previously approved evaluations stay attached to the student forever, no matter where they move.

A student's position was wrong at term start

Admin opens the student profile, chooses Edit, and corrects the current surah and ayah. All future evaluations will start from the corrected position. The admin profile edit is audit logged. Already approved past evaluations are never touched; the historical record is immutable.

Admin needs to close an evaluation early

On the Evaluations page, press Close now on the open round. The window locks immediately. Nothing is auto submitted. Any teacher draft that was not finished stays as a draft. If needed, the admin can re-open the round later with a fresh close date from the Calendar.
