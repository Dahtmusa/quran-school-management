Admin Guide to Qur'an Evaluations

Opening a round, reviewing submissions, approving work and keeping the record clean.

Your job, summarised

You have four duties within the evaluation engine.

  1. Set the Calendar dates for the three evaluations in the current term, in advance.
  2. Press Open on the round when the window is due. Teachers then see it on their dashboards.
  3. Review the submissions as they arrive. Approve a row to lock the student's new position in. Return a row if something needs correcting.
  4. Press Close when the round is finished, or let it run until the Calendar close date.

Where dates live

All evaluation dates come from the School Calendar page. Not from the Evaluations page itself. When you want to change when Eval 1, Eval 2 or Eval 3 opens or closes, you edit it on the Calendar and save. The Evaluations page simply reflects what the Calendar says, and any date change you make will propagate automatically to any open evaluation campaign.

Keep this in mind. The Evaluations page does not ask you for a closing date because the Calendar already knows it. One place, one truth.

Step one. Setting Calendar dates (once per term)

  1. Open the School Calendar page.
  2. Choose the term you are configuring (First Term, Second Term or Third Term for the current academic year).
  3. Scroll to the term's Evaluation Dates section.
  4. For each of the three evaluations, set an Opens date and time and a Closes date and time.
  5. Press Save.

You do not have to configure all three in one sitting. You can set Eval 1 now, run it, and come back later to set Eval 2 before it is due. The system will refuse to open an evaluation if its Calendar date is in the past.

Step two. Opening a round on the day

  1. Open the Evaluations page from the sidebar.
  2. At the top of the page you see three cards, one per round. Each card shows its Calendar close date and the current status (Not Opened, Open or Closed).
  3. Press Open on the round you want to run. The system does everything else in the background.

What the system does when you press Open

  • It creates a fresh evaluation campaign row linked to the Calendar event.
  • It makes sure every active class in the term is attached to the campaign.
  • It creates one draft evaluation row per active student in every attached class.
  • Each draft row stamps the student's current position as the locked starting position. That means a student at At Tahrim ayah 12 will have exactly that as their starting point. Each row is then assigned to the current primary teacher of the student's class.
  • Teachers see the open window and the countdown on their next refresh of their own Evaluations page.

Step three. Reviewing teacher submissions

As teachers press Submit all on their class, their rows arrive in the Pending Teacher Submissions section at the bottom of your Evaluations page. Classes are grouped. Each group shows how many rows are pending and how many were returned to the teacher.

Open a class group and you see one row per student with the following details.

Clicking Approve

When you approve a row, the system does four things in a single transaction.

  1. The evaluation record is marked approved with your identity and the exact approval time.
  2. The student's official current surah, current ayah, current page and current hizb all advance to the stopping position recorded by the teacher.
  3. Their report card is updated immediately. Parents will see the new position on the next load.
  4. A full before and after snapshot of the student record is written into the audit log so the change can be traced any time.

If the student was on their final evaluation of the programme and the stopping position reaches the end of the mushaf in their memorization direction, the system automatically marks the student graduated and issues the completion certificate.

Clicking Return

Returning a row sends it back to the teacher with a short written reason. Use this when something looks wrong and you want the teacher to correct it. Typical reasons include a stopping position that looks too optimistic, an obvious typo in the ayah number, or a comment that needs clarifying. The row's status changes to Returned and reappears on the teacher's grid with a red Returned badge and your written note. The teacher corrects it and resubmits, and the row then returns to your queue.

A returned row is still part of the evaluation. It is not deleted or reset. Only once a row is approved is anything written to the student's permanent position record.

Step four. Closing the round

You can let the round close automatically on the Calendar date, or press Close now on the round card at any time.

  • The window locks. Teachers can no longer submit new rows.
  • Nothing is automatically submitted. Any teacher draft that was not finished stays as a draft on the system.
  • If you need to run the round longer, press Re-open and pick a new close date through the Calendar.

Common situations and how to handle them

I want to open Eval 1 but the Not Opened card refuses to open

The system refuses to open an evaluation whose Calendar close date is in the past or missing. Open the School Calendar, set a close date in the future for that evaluation, save, come back to the Evaluations page and press Open.

A teacher left the school in the middle of a round

Reassign their class to another teacher on the Classes page. On the new teacher's next refresh of their Evaluations page, every pending row for every student in that class will appear in their list. You do not need to re-create the campaign or re-stamp the starting positions. The system matches teachers through the live class teacher link.

A student is sick and misses this round

Leave their row as draft. When the round closes, their row stays unapproved. On the next round, their starting position will be what it was when this round opened, because the system only advances a position when a round is approved. Their journey does not get rewritten.

I accidentally approved the wrong row

Open the student's profile, choose Edit, and correct the position by hand. The admin profile edit is audit logged. If the mistake is big, contact the super admin to use the one off position restore helper. Nothing is ever lost; everything is in the audit log.

Teachers tell me they cannot find the Evaluations page

Check they are using the Teacher sign in, not an admin sign in. Their sidebar should show Evaluations as a menu item. If it does not appear, confirm the staff profile has the teacher role on the Users page.

The scorecard tile on the admin dashboard says Pending Reviews is above zero

That is a reminder that teacher submissions are waiting for your action. Click the tile and work through the Pending Teacher Submissions section until the count returns to zero.

A quick admin checklist, per round

  1. Confirm the School Calendar has sensible open and close dates for Eval 1, Eval 2 and Eval 3 of the current term.
  2. Open the Evaluations page. Press Open on the round whose day has arrived.
  3. Monitor the Pending Reviews tile on the admin dashboard.
  4. Open each submitted class, approve the rows that look correct, return the ones that need a correction with a short reason.
  5. When the round is done, press Close now (or let the Calendar close it automatically).
  6. Repeat for the next round.
