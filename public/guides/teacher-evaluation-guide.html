Teacher's Guide to Entering Evaluations

What to open, what each column means, and how to submit cleanly.

Before you start

Three evaluations are held per term. You will see each one appear on your dashboard the moment the admin opens it. You do not need to do anything to prepare the page; your assigned students will already be listed. Open the Evaluations link on your sidebar and the system shows you only your own class.

Important. Do not record a student's new position by editing their profile from the Students page. The only correct place to record evaluation results is the Evaluations page explained in this guide. Editing from anywhere else will corrupt the student's progress calculation and the next evaluation will fail to open properly.

Finding the evaluation

  1. Sign in to the AMQM teacher dashboard.
  2. On the sidebar, click Evaluations.
  3. You will see three large tiles at the top of the page: Evaluation 1, Evaluation 2 and Evaluation 3. The tile for whichever round is currently open is coloured green and shows a live countdown. The other two show as CLOSED.
  4. Click the open tile. The entry grid for your students appears below.

What the entry grid shows for every student

Each student is a single row. At the top of the row you can see their photo, full name, admission number and class. On the right of the row sits a small chip showing where they stopped last evaluation. That chip is read only; it is their locked starting position for today.

The columns, explained in plain words

ColumnWhat it meansWhat you enter
Starting position (locked) Where this student ended their last approved evaluation. The system has filled it in; you cannot edit it. Nothing. Treat it as the truth and work from there.
Stopping surah The surah the student reached by the end of today's recitation session. Select the surah from the drop down.
Stopping ayah The ayah number within that stopping surah. Type the ayah number.
Mem Memorization. How accurately the student has committed the ayahs to memory, how little hesitation shows. A number from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
Acc Accuracy of pronunciation. Letters coming out of the correct makhraj. A number from 1 to 5.
Flu Fluency. Pace, smoothness, confidence in flow. A number from 1 to 5.
Taj Tajweed. Correct application of the rules of recitation: ghunnah, idgham, madd, qalqalah and so on. A number from 1 to 5.
Ret Retention. How well the student holds the previously memorized portion when you sample it. A number from 1 to 5.
Comment Space for your written observation. Optional. One short line is often enough.
Qur'an complete (score) The system's live calculation of where this student stands in the whole Qur'an at the stopping position you just entered. Nothing. The system updates it as you type.

How to enter a row, step by step

  1. Confirm the locked starting position on the right. If it looks wrong, do not proceed; let the admin know. Starting position corrections are an admin task.
  2. Pick the stopping surah the student reached today.
  3. Type the stopping ayah within that surah.
  4. Set the five rubric scores from one to five. Four is a solid normal score for a student working well.
  5. Optionally add a short comment. A note like "clear madd, needs work on ghunnah" is useful at approval time.
  6. Look at the Qur'an complete chip on the right. If it is showing a percentage, you are good. If it says "Position not moved" in red, the stopping position is wrong for the student's memorization direction. Correct it before moving on.

Understanding the right side chip

Once you have moved the stopping position correctly, the right side chip shows four lines of information.

LineWhat it is telling you
Qur'an complete, in percentHow much of the full Qur'an the student has memorized up to the stopping point you entered.
ayahs out of 6,236The ayah count that produces that percentage. Six thousand two hundred and thirty six is the total number of ayahs in the Qur'an.
pages out of 604The same thing expressed in standard mushaf pages. Six hundred and four is the standard Madani mushaf page count.
This evalJust the chunk the student covered this specific evaluation. For example a line like "+67 ayahs, +3 pages" means the student added that much since the last approved evaluation.
QualityA simple percentage calculated from your five rubric scores. The system records it as a note for the admin. It is not the student's main score.

Direction matters. What the system checks before accepting your row.

Every student has a declared memorization direction. The engine refuses to accept a row where the stopping position has not moved correctly for that direction.

  • If the student is on Baqarah to Nas, their stopping position must be a later point in the mushaf than their starting position.
  • If the student is on Nas to Baqarah, their stopping position must be an earlier point in the mushaf than their starting position.

If the stopping position is at or before the starting position for a forward reader, or at or after it for a reverse reader, the chip will stay on "Position not moved" and the row cannot be submitted.

Submitting your class

You can save a row simply by moving to the next; nothing is sent anywhere until you press the Submit button. When you are done with the whole class, press Submit all at the top or bottom of the grid.

  • Every row is sent to the admin in one action.
  • Each row's status changes from Draft to Pending Approval.
  • Your grid empties. The students you just submitted will not show up again until the admin either approves or returns one.
If a row is returned to you with an admin note, it will reappear in your grid with a Returned badge. Read the admin's reason, correct the row, and press Submit all again. The resubmission goes back to the admin.

Common questions

What if the countdown runs out before I finish?

When the admin closes the window, any row you have not yet submitted stays as a draft. Nothing is automatically submitted or lost. Reach out to the admin; they can reopen the round or accept your remaining submissions through the admin page.

A student was absent today. What do I do?

Leave their row alone. Submit the rows of the students you did evaluate, and let the admin know about the absent one. The admin can reopen the round if needed so you can enter that student later, or they can defer that student to the next evaluation window with a note on the student record.

I think the starting position is wrong for one of my students.

Do not try to fix it by entering a wrong stopping position. Flag the student to the admin. Starting positions are controlled by the student profile, which only the admin can edit, and all edits are logged.

I was reassigned to a class after the window opened. Will I see those students?

Yes. The evaluation engine checks the live class teacher link, so the moment the admin assigns you to a class you will see every pending evaluation row for every student in that class on your next refresh of the Evaluations page.

Can I edit a row after I submit it?

No. Once submitted a row is in the admin's hands. If something needs to change, you can ask the admin to return the row to you with their note, and you can then edit and resubmit.

Quick checklist

  1. Sidebar, click Evaluations.
  2. Click the OPEN tile.
  3. For each student, confirm the starting position, enter the stopping surah and ayah, set the five rubrics, add a short comment if useful.
  4. Make sure every row shows a Qur'an completion percentage, not the red "Position not moved" note.
  5. When the whole class is done, press Submit all.
  6. If a row comes back with a Returned badge, read the reason, correct it, and submit again.
